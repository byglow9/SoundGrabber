<objective>
Implement the two-step promotion flow (D-06/D-07): "Promover" stages a derived featured release in
featured:next and marks the submission promovida — it does NOT publish. A separate explicit
"Publicar" action moves featured:next → featured:current, sending the outgoing featured:current to
featured:history via the existing append helper.

Purpose: D-06 is explicit that promote and publish are distinct actions — collapsing them into one
immediate publish is a locked anti-pattern. The featured document is DERIVED from the submission
(submission = source, featured = derived, D-07), reusing FeaturedReleaseRequest for validation.
Output: promote + publish endpoints turning SUBMIT-06/07 + SEC-SUBMIT-03 (promote/publish half) GREEN.
</objective>
