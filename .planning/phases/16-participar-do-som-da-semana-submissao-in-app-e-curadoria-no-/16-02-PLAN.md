| stored submission → cap eviction | Automated deletion must not destroy active moderation items |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-16-05 | Denial of Service | Oversized submission body → memory exhaustion | mitigate | Tightened Pydantic list caps (artistas 3, produtores 2, links 4) keep realistic bodies under the existing 4KB `_limit_body_size` middleware; no global limit increase |
| T-16-09 | Tampering | Cap eviction deletes non-terminal (Pendente/Promovida) submissions | mitigate | `_enforce_submissions_cap` filters by status, evicting only rejeitada/arquivada; soft-overflow otherwise |
| T-16-04 | Information Disclosure | Contact data persisted alongside public fields | accept (this plan) | Contact stored in the doc but never exposed publicly — the public-response exclusion is enforced at the endpoint layer in Plan 03 (SEC-SUBMIT-05) |
| T-16-02 | Tampering | Malicious field content persisted (XSS payload in descricao/email) | accept (this plan) | Storage persists raw validated text; XSS-safe rendering is enforced where it is displayed (admin tab, Plan 06) |
